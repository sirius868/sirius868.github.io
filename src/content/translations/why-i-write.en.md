title: Why I Still Write Things Down
description: Writing down a question gives my future self a clue to follow.
---

An idea kept only in my head can disappear as soon as the next task arrives. Once I write it down, I can revisit it, question it, or change my mind.

I like to treat a note as a clue, rather than a final answer.
